HarvestFi Project Summary

HarvestFi is a digital platform that helps small farmers get loans without needing land or expensive items as collateral. Farmers receive credit in a digital wallet to buy seeds and tools, and they pay back the loan automatically when they sell their harvest.

By using Interswitch for secure payments, the platform takes a small part of every sale to pay off the debt. This helps farmers stay out of debt while building a digital record that makes it easier for them to get bigger loans in the future.

Evaluation Criteria

Problem Solved
Most small farmers cannot get bank loans because they don't have collateral. This leads to low yields and poverty. HarvestFi solves this by replacing physical collateral with Pay As You Harvest logic. We trust the farmer's future crop and use technology to make repayment automatic.

Technical Execution
The platform is fully functional. We used PHP and MySQL for the backend to manage loans and transactions.
Interswitch API: We integrated Interswitch to handle secure wallet funding and to trigger automatic loan deductions whenever a produce sale is completed.

User Experience
The interface is built with Tailwind CSS to be clean and simple. We focused on a mobile first design because farmers and buyers often use their phones in the field. The dashboard clearly shows the Blinking credit status so users know their account is active and funded.

Innovation
HarvestFi changes how agricultural finance works. Instead of asking for a house deed, we use a Digital Credit Profile. By tracking every sale through our marketplace, we turn a farmer's hard work into their financial reputation.

Completeness
The project is demo ready. All user roles (Admin, Farmer, and Buyer) have working dashboards and can interact with the payment system.
